Instant Alerts

The moment something unusual happens on your property, you're the next to know — not the last.

How Alerts Work

From Detection to Notification, in Moments

When our monitoring team spots something out of place — an unfamiliar face at the door, movement in a restricted area, an entry after hours — the incident is verified immediately and a report is sent your way without delay.

Every alert includes what was seen, when it happened, and which camera caught it, so you have the full picture the moment you're notified.

Why Speed Matters

Faster Alerts Mean Faster Outcomes

An alert that arrives minutes late is far less useful than one that arrives as an event unfolds. That's why our monitoring desk prioritizes verifying and reporting incidents the moment they're confirmed.

Prompt alerts give you the chance to respond, contact authorities, or simply stay informed — while the situation is still current, not after the fact.
