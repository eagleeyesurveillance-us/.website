Real-Time Monitoring

Every camera on your property feeds directly into our live monitoring dashboard, watched by trained eyes around the clock so nothing goes unnoticed.

How It Works

Every Feed, Watched Live — Not Just Recorded

Real-time monitoring means we don't just store footage for later — we actively watch your camera feeds as events unfold. When a driveway camera picks up unexpected movement at 2 a.m. or a warehouse door opens after hours, our monitoring desk sees it happen and responds immediately.

All feeds run through a centralized dashboard where operators can view multiple properties at once, zoom into specific cameras, and cross-reference activity against expected schedules for each site.

What We Cover

Built for Homes, Businesses & Everything Between

Whether it's a single-family home, a retail storefront, or a multi-building warehouse complex, our monitoring setup adapts to the number of cameras and entry points you need covered.

We pay special attention to perimeter access, parking areas, entrances, and any zone you flag as high priority, so the areas that matter most get the closest attention.
