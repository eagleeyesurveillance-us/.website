Who Watches Your Feeds

Trained Operators, Not Just Software

Cameras only capture footage — people are what make that footage useful. Our monitoring team is trained to read a scene quickly: recognizing the difference between a delivery driver, a curious passerby, and someone who genuinely doesn't belong.

Operators work in structured shifts so there's always a fresh, alert set of eyes on your property, backed by clear escalation steps for anything unusual.

How We Prepare Them

Consistency You Can Count On

Every operator follows the same monitoring protocol regardless of shift or property, so the quality of attention your cameras receive never depends on who's on duty that night.

That includes documented response procedures, clear communication steps with clients, and ongoing review of how incidents were handled — so the process keeps improving over time.
